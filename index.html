📥 Input Reception

What's happening: The AI receives your text input as a stream of characters. This is the raw data that will be processed through multiple layers of the neural network.

🔤 Tokenization

Breaking down your question into tokens...

What's happening: The text is broken into "tokens" - smaller units that the AI can understand. Tokens can be words, parts of words, or even punctuation. Each token is converted to a unique number (token ID) that the neural network can process.

What's happening: The tokens flow through multiple layers of artificial neurons. Each layer transforms the data, extracting increasingly abstract patterns and meanings. This is where the "thinking" happens - billions of mathematical operations per second!
4

🎯 Attention & Context

Understanding relationships between words...

Attention weights (which words are most relevant):

What's happening: The attention mechanism helps the AI understand which parts of your question are most important and how different words relate to each other. Brighter cells show stronger connections between words. This allows the AI to grasp context and meaning.
5

📈 Probability Analysis

Calculating probabilities for next words...

What's happening: For each position in the response, the AI calculates probability scores for thousands of possible next tokens. It doesn't just pick the highest probability - it samples from the distribution to create natural, varied responses.
6

✅ Generated Response

Complete response:

What happened: The AI has processed your question through multiple stages: tokenization, neural network processing, attention mechanisms, and probability-based generation. Each response is unique, generated one token at a time based on patterns learned from vast amounts of training data.
